Frequently Asked Questions
How do I reset my password?
Contact your system administrator to reset your password.
How do I apply for leave?
Go to Leave Management → Apply for Leave and fill out the form.
How is payroll processed?
Payroll is run monthly by admins. Go to Payroll → Run Payroll to process.
Who can access configuration?
Only Super Admins can access system configuration settings.
